About Us

We build, manage and support software that helps organisations operate better. From websites and mobile applications to business systems, integrations, security and IT support, we provide practical technology for real-world needs.

What we do.

We combine software engineering, infrastructure and technical support to help organisations build and operate reliable digital systems.

  • Web Applications

    Custom web applications, portals, dashboards and platforms designed around specific business workflows.

  • Mobile Applications

    Android and iOS applications for customers, employees, field teams and organisations that need software on the go.

  • Business Systems

    Management systems that digitise processes such as registration, inspections, compliance, finance, inventory, reporting and operations.

  • APIs & Integrations

    Secure APIs and integrations connecting applications, databases, payment platforms, government services and third-party systems.

  • Security

    Application security, authentication, access control, secure APIs, data protection and security-conscious development practices.

  • IT Support & Maintenance

    Ongoing technical support, system maintenance, monitoring, troubleshooting, updates and improvements for existing software and infrastructure.

Solutions we've built.

Our experience includes software used for public services, compliance, payments, infrastructure, administration and business operations.

  • Government & Public Service Platforms

    Digital platforms supporting registrations, inspections, compliance, permits, reporting and public-service operations.

  • Financial & Payment Systems

    Payment-related platforms, financial workflows, contribution systems, invoicing and digital transaction solutions.

  • Field & Mobile Systems

    Mobile applications and field tools that allow teams to collect information, perform inspections, manage assets and work away from the office.

  • Business Management Systems

    Custom systems for managing business processes, records, users, workflows, documents, reporting and operational information.

Organisations we've worked with.

Our work has supported organisations across telecommunications, government services, finance, infrastructure and technology.

  • Yoya Technologies
  • MTN Uganda
  • NEMA

  • National Lotteries & Gaming Regulatory Board
  • GDExperts

Building is only part of the job.

We also help organisations keep their software running, secure and useful long after the original development work is complete.

  • System Maintenance

    Bug fixes, upgrades, performance improvements and continued development as business requirements change.

  • Technical Support

    Troubleshooting, user support, system investigations and technical assistance for day-to-day operations.

  • Monitoring & Infrastructure

    Monitoring applications and infrastructure, investigating failures and helping maintain reliable deployments.

  • Security & Updates

    Security improvements, dependency updates, access management and ongoing attention to application security.

Based in Kampala. Working beyond Kampala.

We work with organisations in Kampala and across Uganda, while supporting projects and clients beyond Uganda through remote collaboration.
